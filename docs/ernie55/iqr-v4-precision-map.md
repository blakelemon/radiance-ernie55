## Why

- **vector_or_scalar** (f32): A norm gain, bias, sink, mHC alpha/base/scale, correction bias, per-expert scale or MultiMax table: a vector or scalar that no GEMM reads, tiny, and the plugin reads at its stored width. Kept exactly as shipped. Cites: docs/ARCHITECTURES.md:628
- **mhc_mixing** (f32): The mHC mixing matrices feed a 20-iteration Sinkhorn and the residual stream mixing of every layer; an error here is multiplied through all 43 layers. Radiance stores the flagship model's mixing matrices as e4m3 rows or bf16 under its own fused read; ERNIE's ernie_mhc_pre_rms reads them f32 and has no device row in any format, and they are 0.26 GiB (0.2% of the model), so they stay f32 (rule: cast dtype=f32, the transpose the plugin already does). bf16 is the fallback once a kernel exists and the Sinkhorn sensitivity is measured. Cites: docs/ARCHITECTURES.md:624, data/recipes/qwen4exp-u4-w6nl-bf16ple.recipe:32, data/recipes/ernie55-weights.recipe:11, docs/ernie55/device-dispatch.md:13
- **hca_compressor** (f32): The compressor's absolute position bias [128, 512] f32, 9 MiB for all layers, read by ernie_hca_compress. Kept f32. Cites: docs/ernie55/device-dispatch.md:13
- **embedding** (bf16): The embedding gather feeds every layer and costs 1.5 GiB bf16 (a 4-bit embedding costs IQ8R +0.00058 KL on the 27B, 6-bit +0.00003; docs/IQR.md). Radiance keeps embed_tokens bf16 for the flagship MoE. embed_lookup_q / embed_lookup_iqr exist if the bytes are wanted later (n_embd 4096 is a multiple of 128). Cites: docs/ARCHITECTURES.md:628, docs/IQR.md:79
- **vector_or_scalar** (bf16): A norm gain, bias, sink, mHC alpha/base/scale, correction bias, per-expert scale or MultiMax table: a vector or scalar that no GEMM reads, tiny, and the plugin reads at its stored width. Kept exactly as shipped. Cites: docs/ARCHITECTURES.md:628
- **hca_compressor** (bf16): The HCA compressor's kv and score projections (512 x 4096, bf16, read f32) build the compressed cache every 128 tokens; their error accumulates in the fp32 compressor state and is carried by every later token. 296 MiB in total: kept as shipped.
